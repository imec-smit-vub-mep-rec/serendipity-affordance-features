# Incompleteness

**Serendipity affordance family:** Diversifiability  
**Definition:** Unfinalizable potentials, for example broad, ambiguous, or evolving categories.

Incompleteness leaves interpretive room in the information environment. Features mapped here can stimulate serendipity by keeping categories, tags, or user traces open enough that users can form unexpected associations rather than only follow predetermined classifications.
