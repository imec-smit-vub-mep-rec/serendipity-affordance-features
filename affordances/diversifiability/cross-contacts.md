# Cross-Contacts

**Serendipity affordance family:** Diversifiability  
**Definition:** Colliding potentials, for example different domains, genres, or users meeting in one environment.

Cross-contacts describe moments where otherwise separate items, people, genres, lists, or domains are brought into contact. Features mapped here can stimulate serendipity by placing unfamiliar materials near familiar ones or by letting social and editorial traces collide with the user's current path.
