# Contrast

**Serendipity affordance family:** Sensoriability  
**Definition:** Highlighting sharper or more sudden cues, for example, contrasting backgrounds.

Contrast concerns sudden or sharp perceptual difference. Features mapped here can stimulate serendipity by helping an item, media element, or interruption stand out from the surrounding interface.
