# Pointers

**Serendipity affordance family:** Sensoriability  
**Definition:** Narrow, specific cues such as signs, labels, prompts, or headings, for example, banners.

Pointers are specific cues that tell users where something is, why it appears, or what path can be followed next. Features mapped here can stimulate serendipity by making possible routes and relations legible at the moment of choice.
