# Multi-Reachability

**Serendipity affordance family:** Traversability  
**Definition:** Multiple routes between spots or items, for example, multi-list interfaces.

Multi-reachability concerns the number of routes between items and places in the information environment. Features mapped here can stimulate serendipity by letting users arrive at the same item through tags, people, lists, recommendations, links, or social traces.
