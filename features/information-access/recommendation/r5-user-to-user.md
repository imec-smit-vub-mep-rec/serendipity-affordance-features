# R5 User-To-User Recommendation

**Category:** Information access  
**Subcategory:** Recommendation  
**Affordance mapping:** Cross-contacts, Multi-reachability, Explorability, Pointers

User-to-user recommendations connect a user to other users, profiles, reviewers, followers, taste neighbors, or community members whose activity can lead to items.

## Why It May Support Serendipity

Other people create routes into unfamiliar parts of the catalogue. Their ratings, lists, reviews, or profiles can expose unexpected items and make social traces explorable.

## Design Considerations

- Decide whether the system recommends people, items through people, or both.
- Show enough context to explain why another user is relevant.
- Consider privacy, consent, and visibility of profile or activity data.
