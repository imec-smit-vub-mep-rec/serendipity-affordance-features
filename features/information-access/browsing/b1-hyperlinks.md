# B1 Hyperlinks

**Category:** Information access  
**Subcategory:** Browsing  
**Affordance mapping:** Accessibility, Multi-reachability, Explorability

Hyperlinks are clickable paths between items, creators, tags, categories, lists, profiles, collections, reviews, or other entities.

## Why It May Support Serendipity

Hyperlinks create multiple routes through the environment. They let users leave a planned path and follow an association, which can increase exploratory movement and unplanned encounters.

## Design Considerations

- Make meaningful entities clickable where they can support exploration.
- Keep link styling recognizable and consistent.
- Track path data to understand how users move from planned to divergent routes.
