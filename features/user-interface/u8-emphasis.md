# U8 Emphasis

**Category:** User interface  
**Affordance mapping:** Exposure, Contrast

Emphasis covers visual or auditory treatments that make an item, cue, or area stand out, such as size, color, placement, badges, motion, highlighting, or priority ordering.

## Why It May Support Serendipity

Emphasis can increase exposure to selected items and create contrast against surrounding material. This may help users notice items they would otherwise pass over.

## Design Considerations

- Identify whether emphasis is based on relevance, novelty, editorial choice, popularity, or commercial priority.
- Avoid overusing emphasis, since everything highlighted becomes nothing highlighted.
- Record whether emphasized items receive more attention, saves, clicks, or serendipity reports.
