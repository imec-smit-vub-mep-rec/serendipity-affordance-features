# U2 User-Generated Content

**Category:** User interface  
**Affordance mapping:** Slowability, Multi-reachability

This feature concerns the presentation of user-generated content in the interface: visible reviews, ratings, tags, comments, lists, or other social traces.

## Why It May Support Serendipity

User-generated traces can encourage slower inspection and create additional routes through the catalogue, especially when tags, reviewers, or lists are clickable.

## Design Considerations

- Show enough context to make user contributions interpretable.
- Make social traces navigable when they can lead to other items or people.
- Separate aggregate signals from individual voices when both are present.
