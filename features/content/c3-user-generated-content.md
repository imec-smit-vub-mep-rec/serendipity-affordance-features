# C3 User-Generated Content

**Category:** Content  
**Affordance mapping:** Diversity, Cross-contacts, Incompleteness, Multi-reachability

User-generated content includes tags, reviews, ratings, lists, comments, uploaded items, annotations, and other traces contributed by users.

## Why It May Support Serendipity

User contributions add perspectives that the official catalogue may not contain. Tags and reviews can create cross-contacts between users, genres, themes, and items, while informal or incomplete traces can invite unexpected interpretation.

## Design Considerations

- Preserve the difference between editorial metadata and user-contributed signals.
- Make tags, ratings, and reviews navigable when they can serve as alternative routes.
- Moderate or qualify noisy user content without removing the useful messiness that can support discovery.
